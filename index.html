    Rate bands

    One band per rule. Up to 30. The highest Pays % that matches a slab is the one that wins.

    How a slab is priced

    The best band wins
    The bot checks every band and uses the highest Pays % among the ones that match. If two tie, it takes the bigger payout.
    Ranges are over From, up to To
    A $100 card belongs to a 0 to 100 band, not to a 100 to 1,000 band. Leave To blank for no ceiling.
    No band means no offer
    A slab that fits no band is not priced. The seller is told "We're not buying this right now" and staff can still quote it by hand.
    CGC is its own category
    A CGC slab only matches bands set to CGC. A band set to Any PSA never takes one, so leave at least one CGC band in place if you buy CGC.
    New certs means 70,000,000 and up
    Cert rule "New certs 7+" only takes PSA cert numbers of 70,000,000 or higher.
    Vintage and Special
    Vintage holo only: the card year is at or under the vintage year above and the card name has "holo" in it. Special or WOTC only: the card year is at or under the vintage year, or the name contains one of the special keywords above.
    Basis is what the percent is taken from
    CL value is the Card Ladder value. Avg of last 3 is the average of the last three sales. Lowest of the two is whichever is smaller. "Use default" follows the Default basis at the top, and the per-seller Market pick staff make in Discord beats both.
    Language
    Any takes every slab. Pick one language and the band only takes slabs read as that language. A slab with no readable card name only fits Any.
